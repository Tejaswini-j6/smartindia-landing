About SmartIndia.ai

Making information smarter, simpler and more accessible.

SmartIndia.ai is an AI-powered platform designed to bring people and artificial intelligence closer together.

Our goal is to make discovering information more intelligent, convenient and accessible. By combining AI capabilities with multiple sources of information, SmartIndia.ai helps users explore the digital world through a smarter search experience.

Founded in 2025 and based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, SmartIndia.ai is focused on building technology that makes AI more useful in everyday life — and on engineering the dynamic websites, e-commerce systems and multi-vendor platforms that carry our clients' businesses online.

Mission

To make AI-powered information discovery simple, accessible and useful for everyone.

Vision

To build an intelligent digital ecosystem where people can seamlessly interact with information through AI.
